# University Startups

> We make special education teachers' lives easier. One AI-powered platform that automates federally mandated IEP transition planning while reducing teacher workload by 70%.

University Startups is a Transition Planning Management platform built exclusively for special education teams. It helps teachers create IDEA-compliant IEP transition plans in under 10 minutes, gives administrators a compliance reviewer that audits plans against 11 IDEA criteria in under 90 seconds, and engages students directly in building their own transition plans.

## Core Products

- **Transition Data Hub**: The educator's command center. Centralizes caseload management, student portfolios, and IEP calendars in one workspace. Features AI document ingestion that parses existing IEPs to auto-populate new plans instantly.

- **Transition Plan Creator**: A student-facing AI assistant. Uses adaptive, 10-minute conversations to help students with disabilities build their own IDEA-compliant transition plans, ensuring student voice is at the heart of every IEP.

- **Interactive Goal Bank**: A professional tool for teachers to generate measurable, SMART-format IEP goals. Input a student's grade and performance level, receive personalized goals in seconds.

- **Transition Plan Reviewer**: An AI compliance engine that audits IEP transition plans against 11 IDEA criteria in under 90 seconds. Flags compliance gaps and provides specific fix recommendations so every plan is airtight before the meeting.

- **Transition Readiness Score**: A collection of 5-minute surveys for students, parents, and teachers that generate a Transition Readiness Score, measuring growth and establishing baseline preparedness.

- **Career & Classroom Tools**: Includes a College & Career Readiness (CCR) curriculum with AI learning assistant **Vic**, and an Internship Simulator with dedicated AI coach **Nova**.

## Key Claims

- 70% reduction in teacher documentation time (from 4 to 6 hours per student to under 10 minutes)
- Compliance review in under 90 seconds against 11 IDEA criteria
- 88% of students feel more hopeful about their futures
- 60-Day Teacher Promise: full money-back guarantee if teachers' lives aren't easier in 60 days

## Audience

- **Special education teachers**: Creating IDEA-compliant IEP transition plans, writing SMART goals, tracking student progress, managing caseloads
- **Students with disabilities**: Building their own transition plans with AI-guided support, exploring career and college readiness
- **District administrators and compliance officers**: Ensuring IDEA compliance across all plans, reviewing documents before IEP meetings
- **Parents**: Participating in their child's transition assessment and planning process
